# Accept hosted production integrations and page coverage

Planning snapshot, 16 October 2026. [Live issue](https://linear.app/vaper/issue/GRS-66/accept-hosted-production-integrations-and-page-coverage) is the execution authority.

## Outcome

Aggregate acceptance of the production capabilities promised for hosted alpha. Preserve the original product scope and map each route to a concrete owner; complete only when the alpha owners are independently accepted.

## Alpha owners

* [Implement app-side email drafts and firm-managed templates](<https://linear.app/vaper/issue/GRS-126/implement-app-side-email-drafts-and-firm-managed-templates>)
* [Implement represented-client KYC review and separate compliance access](<https://linear.app/vaper/issue/GRS-124/implement-represented-client-kyc-review-and-separate-compliance-access>)
* [Carry case costs, timesheets and disbursements into the hosted app](<https://linear.app/vaper/issue/GRS-125/carry-case-costs-timesheets-and-disbursements-into-the-hosted-app>)
* [Implement permission-scoped audit events and audit views](<https://linear.app/vaper/issue/GRS-116/implement-permission-scoped-audit-events-and-audit-views>)
* [Require explicit privilege confirmation and disclosure scope review](<https://linear.app/vaper/issue/GRS-127/require-explicit-privilege-confirmation-and-disclosure-scope-review>)
* [Persist PDF bundles with pinned inputs and protected version history](<https://linear.app/vaper/issue/GRS-128/persist-pdf-bundles-with-pinned-inputs-and-protected-version-history>)

Identity/team/cases/support are owned by the foundation; client intake, queue/download, private import/editing and exact-version publication retain their named delivery issues. [Notify uploaders of publication and required input without blocking access](<https://linear.app/vaper/issue/GRS-129/notify-uploaders-of-publication-and-required-input-without-blocking>) supplies client notices. Newer emails/silences/anchors/organogram/WhatsApp/partial columns remain with export compatibility, not an invented new integration.

## Accepted behavior

Exported provider-neutral Outlook/Gmail email files and app-side drafts/templates are alpha scope; live mailbox connections, provider-side drafts and sync stay in the explicit later integration issue and do not block alpha. KYC is manual represented-client review, separate compliance grants and warning-only incompleteness. Billing means current case costs, not Pay ClearCase. Audit visibility is role/case scoped. Privilege confirmation is explicit; disclosure defaults exclude confirmed privileged files with visible editor override, while complete backups preserve their scope. Bundles pin durable inputs and versions; keep all by default, optional latest+N prior unpinned, Keep/source protection.

## Coverage and verification

Own docs/acceptance/integrations/index.md, a route-by-route ownership/evidence reconciliation of docs/reference/route-map.md, and independent integrated outcome checks. Shared production feature paths belong to the child owners; do not become another implementation writer. Record unknown production slugs as coverage gaps to investigate, not invented features or automatic exclusions. Original navigation remains; Figma/UX redesign is later.

Verify every alpha route/capability at the exact integrated SHA with appropriate permissions, durable assets and recovery. Explicit later-scope owners are listed separately. The final hosted gate remains responsible for the complete release verdict.

All child work remains behind the overall hosted-alpha planning gate. Use synthetic fixtures, pin the accepted upstream SHA, retain independent review and exact-revision receipts, and keep current production/RAWS read-only. This planning realignment dispatches no app work.
