# Hosted design records

The [GRS-26 integration record](acceptance/hosting/index.md) joins the six local and Clerk development proofs. It records their exact source revisions, contracts, numeric limits and remaining real-provider legs. The [CC-26 packet](acceptance/CC-26/README.md) is the independent design review guide. Neither page records an integrated design verdict.

The accepted decisions are [client isolation and identity](adr/0007-hosted-client-isolation-and-identity.md), [Workers and the Cloudflare-first stack](adr/0008-workers-runtime-and-cloudflare-first-stack.md), [candidate import](adr/0009-manual-queue-and-candidate-import-contract.md), [publication](adr/0010-exact-version-signoff-and-publication.md) and [originals and viewing](adr/0011-originals-and-complete-in-app-file-viewing.md). The [viewer contract](acceptance/viewers/contract.md) supplies the accepted format defaults and the proof still owed for hosted delivery.
