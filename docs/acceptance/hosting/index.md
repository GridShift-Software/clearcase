## Load envelope and numeric limits

The initial load model is one or two isolated clients. The synthetic v5 case has **1,689 document rows**, **1,662 originals**, **3,211,390,882 original bytes**, a **258,590,305-byte** largest original, and **37 package files totalling 6,249,551 bytes**. The 2,131-document export is a later test. The storage probe exercised 100 concurrent user-work adds, 64 MiB multipart upload parts, and an export of 385 steps with one 8 MiB part per step. These are workload and implementation measurements, not remote capacity claims. [Synthetic corpus](storage.md#the-synthetic-corpus), [storage legs](storage.md#legs), [ADR 0008 workload](../../adr/0008-workers-runtime-and-cloudflare-first-stack.md#what-the-user-confirmed-on-14-october).

The fixed operating limits are **USD 25 per month** for the cloud proof, with an alert at **USD 10**; **one-hour RPO** for recent Notes and case edits, **30-day** recovery-copy rotation, and a **four staffed-hour** restore aim. The last three need M2 cloud evidence. The notification probe caps retries at **three attempts**, after **5** and **30 minutes**, with a default **10-second** send timeout. The deployment janitor's local policy waits **24 hours**. [GRS-26 plan](../../plan/tickets/CC-15.md), [recovery acceptance](recovery.md#acceptance-items), [notification retry](notifications.md#the-design-under-test), [janitor](deployment.md#disposable-resources).

**HOLD: numeric hosted latency, concurrency, CPU and memory budgets.** GRS-106 and GRS-109 explicitly defer them to M2 P1–P3 on real Workers, D1 and R2. Local times, including a 24.2 ms cold document page and 3.3 ms warm page, are observations on local workerd and cannot set hosted budgets. The gate must approve a numeric staging budget against the envelope above before client intake uses it. [Runtime HOLD](runtime.md#what-stays-hold), [storage measurements](storage.md#local-measurements-not-budgets).

For M2 planning, the **proposed staging targets** carry forward the local pilot's p95 ceilings: usable overview **2,000 ms**, warm navigation **200 ms**, search **300 ms**, and user-work save/read **200 ms**, measured from a South African vantage on the v5-size fixture. These are candidate targets for the gate, not measured hosted performance or approved hosted budgets. Record cold-isolate fraction, Workers CPU and memory, upload/finalise throughput, and export/restore times before setting their hosted ceilings. [Pilot budget](../../plan/tickets/CC-03.md), [search budget](../../plan/tickets/CC-12.md), [user-work budget](../../plan/tickets/CC-07.md), [storage HOLD](storage.md#what-stays-hold).

- The real Cloudflare P10 deployment, remote limits, EU placement, Time Travel, recovery timing and Email Sending remain pinned at M1–M3. Real-provider acceptance is mandatory. The [design-gate packet](../CC-26/README.md) names their owning tickets and refusal scenarios.
- The chosen one-D1 layout lacks the remote two-D1 comparison. The storage proof records unresolved `head` then write races, a restore race after its user-work batch, and the large-object delivery residual. [Layout](storage.md#the-layout-frozen-for-product-owners), [interfaces](storage.md#frozen-interfaces).
- Clerk production cookie scope and CSP, a second client application's wrong-issuer test, actual provider-session revocation bound, and full audit completeness remain open. [Identity HOLD](identity.md#what-stays-hold), [ADR 0007](../../adr/0007-hosted-client-isolation-and-identity.md#still-open).
- The old production operator results-import endpoint is **unverified**. Client intake upload is a different API. Build the new candidate import against the [results-package contract](../../results-package.md) and [ADR 0009](../../adr/0009-manual-queue-and-candidate-import-contract.md); defer legacy migration until [CC-14 discovery](../../plan/tickets/CC-14.md) obtains authorised evidence.
