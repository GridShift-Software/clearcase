Only diffs under `src/lib/auth`, `src/lib/tenancy`, the sign-in routes and the grant producers get a security review, and that review is the one review. Lows go to `follow-ups.md` unread.

Pass: every page under 2 s, originals under 3 s, no 401 or 503, under 8 minutes total. Record SHA, times and URL in status. If it fails, nothing else moves. `gate` and `gate:hosted` run after merge, nightly, never before a deploy.
**Deploy cadence.** A staging Worker takes every merged head after targeted tests, at least twice a day. Client Workers A and B take the previous evening's staging head once its journey passed. Rollback is redeploying the recorded SHA. Every deploy re-applies the placement pin (a plain `wrangler deploy` resets it).

Blocked or needs the user (at most 5). Next (at most 5). Spend (1 line). No pins, hashes, receipts or history; those go under `reports/`.
**Daily human use.** The owner, Dion or Karlou opens staging daily on a laptop and a phone for ten minutes along the journey above and writes three lines (slow, broken, wanted) in `.orchestration/human-log.md`. ## 4. Why the owner kept adding process, and six rules
Pattern: each pain got a permanent rule and no rule was ever retired. RETRO-20261012 (Dian's 9 findings missed) added REVIEW.md probes; the 17 Oct threat model added 13 hosted classes and `gate:hosted` on every diff; a probe that failed a gate on 19 Oct added scratch checkouts; GRS-22's lock bug added fix re-checks; a leak added `leak-scan` and `push-approved`; a missed PR comment added merge records and `merge-check`; model-cost worry on 9–10 Oct added orders 13–21 in `preferences.md` about max and xhigh; the 23 Oct "20 sub edges" added a batch-mode section, then a full rewrite on 25 Oct. Word counts: AGENTS 1,091 (17 Oct) → 2,912 (25 Oct bak); ORCHESTRATOR 428 → 2,478; REVIEW 530 → 1,996. Every rule guards a past mistake; none measures the live product. The owner asked for production assurance on a synthetic alpha and never wrote down where speed beats proof, so the agents' bar only rose (why-slow-claude, "What is being done wrong").
1. Add a rule only by deleting one. Cap the process files (AGENTS 800 words, ORCHESTRATOR 500, REVIEW 500).
2. The first deliverable each week is a URL you open yourself. If you cannot use it, no review, guard or ADR counts as progress.
3. Set the bar as numbers before work starts (p95 page time, one review, two fix rounds). A finding outside the numbers is a follow-up, not a round.
4. Write down once where speed beats proof for this stage. Agents cannot make that trade for you; left alone they choose assurance.
5. On a third round of the same unit, change the premise, not the model (session renewal took five rounds on "no clerk-js" before anyone asked).
6. Read the 30-line status and the three human-log lines yourself every day. If the state takes more than two minutes to read, the project has become the process.
